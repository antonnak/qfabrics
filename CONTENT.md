## How We Work

**Headline:** How we work

**Subhead:** From first call to production deployment in 6-8 weeks. Here's what that looks like.

### Process
1. **Week 1: Discovery** - Map workflows, identify automation targets, define success metrics, assess data
2. **Week 2: Architecture** - Design agent system, select components, plan integrations, define checkpoints
3. **Weeks 3-6: Build** - Develop core functionality, integrate data sources, test, iterate
4. **Weeks 6-8: Deploy & Support** - Deploy to production, train team, monitor, handoff docs

### What we need from you
- A sponsor who can make decisions
- Access to relevant data and systems
- 2-3 hours per week for feedback and reviews
- Willingness to iterate

### FAQs
**Do you work with our existing tech stack?**
Yes. We integrate with whatever you're running—Salesforce, HubSpot, internal databases, cloud providers. We build in your environment, not ours.

**What about data security?**
Your data stays in your infrastructure. We build systems that run in your cloud, behind your security perimeter. We sign strict NDAs and IP agreements. The system is yours.

**What if the AI makes mistakes?**
It will, especially early on. That's why we design human-in-the-loop checkpoints for critical decisions. The AI handles the 80% that's routine; your team focuses on the 20% requiring judgment.

**How is this different from ChatGPT or Copilot?**
Those are general-purpose assistants. We build specialized agents trained on your workflows, integrated with your systems, optimized for your use cases. ChatGPT can summarize a document—our agents run your entire process end-to-end.

**We have an internal AI team. Why do we need you?**
We work alongside internal teams. They focus on infrastructure and company-wide initiatives; we focus on business-specific agents in domains like talent and investment research. We accelerate your roadmap without competing for resources.
